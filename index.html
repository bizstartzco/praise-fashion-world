The idea

Fewer pieces. Better ones.

Praise Fashion World is menswear for the way men in Nepal actually dress: office in the morning, tea with friends after, a wedding or a night out at the weekend.

So we keep the collection tight. Shirts that hold their shape, jackets that layer over them, and a few sharper pieces for when it matters.

  • 01Modern cuts, not costume. Clothes that work together.
  • 02Colours you can wear together, so one jacket works with every shirt.
  • 03Real people answer your messages, before and after you buy.

How to order

Three steps. No checkout.

01

Pick your piece

Open any item in the shop, choose your size and how many you want.

02

Send the message

Tap the order button. Your order opens in WhatsApp already typed out. Just press send.

03

We confirm

We reply to confirm stock, the final price and delivery to your address, then pack your order.
