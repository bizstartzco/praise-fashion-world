## 2026-10-27: Multi-page site, shoes, animations

**Done**
- **Multi-page:** home, shop, about, contact, plus **17 product pages** (`products/<id>.html`),
  each with its own title, description and share preview.
- **Shoes:** 7 shoes added (brogue, tassel loafer, derby, white high-top, white low sneaker, lace-up
  boot, work boot). They get a "Shoes" link in the nav, a tile in Collections, a filter in the
  shop, and a scrollable "Finish the fit" rail on the home page.
- **Product page:** size picker (EU sizes for shoes), quantity, WhatsApp order with size, quantity,
  price and page link pre-typed, details accordion, and "You may also like".
- **Animations:**
  - Hero: masked headline reveal, image wipe and parallax
  - Scroll: staggered fade-ups and image wipes
  - Header: hides on scroll down, returns on scroll up
  - Menu: full-screen mobile menu with a staggered entrance
  - Hover: fills on buttons and card zoom with a slide-up "View piece" bar
  - Product page: image zoom that follows the pointer, and a size-picker shake if no size is chosen
  - Page changes: cross-page View Transitions, with the product photo morphing from card to page
  - All motion is off under reduce-motion
- **Build:** pages are generated by `node tools/build.js` from `src/`, so the product list lives in one file.
- **Checks:** 687 internal links and images resolve, with no missing files. Each of the 21 pages
  has a unique title and description. Screenshots taken at desktop and phone widths.

## 2026-10-27: First build
