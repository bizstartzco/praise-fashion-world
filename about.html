About

Dress well,every day.

Why we started

Menswear without the guesswork.

Most men we know own too many clothes they never wear and not enough they actually like. Praise Fashion World is our answer: a short, considered collection of modern menswear and shoes that works together.

Every piece is chosen to fit the way men in Nepal live: office days, long commutes, tea with friends, family functions, weddings, and weekends out. Clean cuts, colours that match each other, and nothing you have to think too hard about.

We sell directly, through a conversation. You message us, we answer, and we help you find the right size before anything is packed.
